# Synthetic data and planted stories

All data is generated by `sql/setup.sql` with a seeded hash (`RAW.U(i, key)`), so every run creates the
same rows. Nothing comes from a real bank, card program, or customer. Names of people and merchants are
invented; city coordinates are public city centers. The `lib/mock/sample.json` bundle used by Sample
mode was captured from a Live run of the app on this data, including AI_COMPLETE and Cortex Agent text.

| Table | Rows (approx.) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CUSTOMERS`, `ACCOUNTS`, `CARDS` | 60,012 each | One checking account and one debit card per customer, plus 12 new mule accounts |
| `MERCHANTS` | 2,003 | 60 in-store merchants per city in 30 US cities, 200 online, 3 used by the stories |
| `TRANSACTIONS` | 2,971,547 | Oct 1 to Nov 14, 2026: card present (55%), card not present (30%), P2P instant payments (15%) |
| `ACCOUNT_EVENTS` | 78 | Logins, password reset, payees added |
| `DEVICES`, `IP_ADDRESSES` | derived | First seen and number of accounts per device or IP |
| `CHARGEBACKS` | 2,983 | Fraud labels: chargebacks, plus issuer-confirmed card-testing authorizations |

Background fraud (about 0.1% of payments) uses an unfamiliar device and IP, larger amounts, and
night-time hours, so the model has real signal to learn. "Now" in the data is 2026-11-15 09:00.

## Planted stories

| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Card testing.** 420 card-not-present authorizations of $0.50 to $1.99 on 420 different cards at *Zentrova Digital Goods* between 02:10 and 03:40 on Nov 12, from four bot devices; 18 of the cards cash out the next day. | Card-testing rule, alert `AL90000200`; the Ask screen's first question |
| 2 | **Account takeover.** Account `A012345` logs in from new device `DATO1`, resets its password, adds three payees, then sends six P2P transfers totaling $9,800 in 34 minutes on Nov 14. | Top of the open queue, case `AL9200001` |
| 3 | **Mule ring.** Twelve new accounts (`A060001`-`A060012`) share three devices and two IPs, take P2P inflows, move money among themselves, and cash out in gift cards. The takeover device also logs in to one mule account, so the takeover case's link graph reaches the ring. | Case `AL9400084`; link graph on case `AL9200001` |
| 4 | **Impossible travel.** Card `C034560` (home: Boston) swipes in Boston at 14:02 on Nov 13, then in Miami 28 minutes later, 2,019 km away by `ST_DISTANCE`. | Case `AL9500002` |
| 5 | **False-positive cluster.** A legitimate promotion at *Quillmont Outfitters* on Nov 8 brings 950 checkouts at $149 or $179, many from shoppers' new phones. The model flags 434 of them; none are fraud. | Overview chart on Nov 8, False positive tab, case `AL9600948` |
